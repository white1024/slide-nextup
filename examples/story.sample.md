## Goal and audience

The goal of this proposal is to get the team to agree to make one real proposal deck with the new workflow next week. The audience make their own slides; what hurts most is revising the layout until their patience runs out, yet nobody has traced the problem back to "the story was never confirmed". They need to see evidence and a low-risk next step.

## Core message

Most of the time we spend revising slides goes into the layout, and the layout keeps changing because the story was never confirmed first. Put the story gate before production and the layout changes disappear.

## Narrative skeleton

1. Pain: the time spent revising slides all goes into the layout.
2. Diagnosis: the tool is not missing features; the order is wrong.
3. Solution: confirm the story first, then produce and edit.
4. Evidence: two trial decks reached zero rounds of layout revision.
5. Action: use it for one real proposal next week.

### s3 | The truth about revision rounds
- scene_role: evidence
- intensity: 3
- content_relation: evidence
- message: Of three layout rounds, two came from a content order change midway.
- evidence:
  - A round-by-round log of six decks and twenty-four rounds of revision
  - Sixteen rounds concerned the layout; eleven began with a change in content order
- notes: Stress that "the layout got changed" is the result, not the cause.

### s4 | The tool is fine, the order is wrong
- scene_role: evidence
- intensity: 3
- content_relation: comparison
- message: Every tool we have makes you lay out the slides first and think about the story afterwards, so each change to the story means laying out again.
- evidence:
  - "Today: open the slide app, lay out, present once, notice the order is wrong, lay out again"
  - "Goal: write the story, confirm, generate the layout, fine-tune only"
- notes: Let the two columns speak for themselves; do not read them out.

### s5 | The five steps of the new workflow
- scene_role: relationship
- intensity: 4
- content_relation: sequence
- message: The new workflow puts "confirm the story" before generating the layout; after that only fine-tuning remains.
- evidence: brief -> story document (you confirm it) -> visual direction -> generate -> edit in the browser
- notes: Point out that "you confirm it" is the only step where a person has to stop.

- notes: This is the peak slide; make the numbers big and stay on it longer.
