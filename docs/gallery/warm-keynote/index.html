Warm cream paper with a faint 48px grid, dark brown text, teal as the only accent and orange-red reserved for consequences and risks. White glass cards (32px radius, large soft shadows), pill-shaped process nodes, section heads with a short teal bar and monospace text, Inter 900 big numbers, tables inside glass containers. Headings and body are Noto Sans TC (a local font), Latin and numerals use Inter, furniture uses JetBrains Mono. The style comes from a friend’s internal deck (with their consent, no attribution required); none of its content is used.
