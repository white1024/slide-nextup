Technical Brief technical-brief

A dark technical brief: a near-black green-grey room, panels one step up from the ground with a single hairline border and no shadow, a monospace uppercase eyebrow in bright teal trailed by a thin rule, dash-marked lists under a coloured pip label, monospace tabular numbers, and a teal hairline progress bar along the top edge. Teal is the working accent; emerald and amber mark the second and third card of a triple, and amber alone means a caveat. Ported from the author's own hand-written 1280x720 architecture deck, geometry x1.5; every face is a system font, because a projector is not guaranteed to have a network and a failed web font falls back silently.
